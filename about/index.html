About AGX Couriers Ltd.

Fast, reliable, trusted shipping support.

AGX Couriers Ltd. helps customers manage online shopping, package forwarding, mail forwarding, product sourcing, and delivery coordination with clear communication and careful handling.

Every shipment matters. Whether you are sending important documents, receiving personal packages, or managing business shipments, our goal is to make the process simple, secure, and stress-free.

  • Swift service for customers who need dependable updates.
  • Trusted delivery from warehouse arrival to pickup or delivery.
  • Helpful support for shoppers, families, and businesses.

Our Promise

We deliver with care and speed.

Our service is built for people who want online shopping and shipping to feel less complicated. You can use your AGX Couriers Ltd. shipping address, receive arrival notifications, track package movement, and arrange the pickup or delivery option that works for you.

Packages are shipped from the overseas warehouse two times per week, on Wednesdays and Saturdays. Payment is accepted by cash or bank transfer.
